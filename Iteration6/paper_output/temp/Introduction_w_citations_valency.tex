Metastable materials, which reside in local minima on the potential energy landscape above the thermodynamic ground state, represent a rich frontier for discovering novel functionalities.\citep{Du_2025,Sun_2016} These non-equilibrium phases can host unique electronic, catalytic, and optical properties that are inaccessible in their stable counterparts.\citep{Du_2025} However, a fundamental challenge often hinders their practical realization: the energetic penalty of metastability frequently correlates with mechanical fragility.\citep{Du_2025,aykol2019thermodynamic} This inherent instability poses a significant barrier to the synthesis, isolation, and ultimate application of these promising materials, making their discovery a resource-intensive and often serendipitous process.\citep{Du_2025,aykol2019thermodynamic,Sun_2016}

Transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) exemplify this paradigm. While their stable semiconducting polymorphs are well-established platforms for next-generation electronics, their metastable phases, such as the metallic 1T and 1T' structures, are highly coveted for applications ranging from electrocatalysis to quantum information.\citep{Kretschmer_2017,yin2024unraveling,Wang_2020,Lee_2020,Yin_2021,Demirkol_2022,Li_2026} The synthesis of these functional metastable TMDs requires a delicate balance, as many theoretically promising candidates are too mechanically soft to be experimentally isolated.\citep{yin2024unraveling,Demirkol_2022,Li_2026} The vast combinatorial space defined by different transition metals and chalcogens makes an exhaustive search for mechanically robust candidates via either first-principles calculations or experimental trial-and-error computationally intractable.\citep{hemmat2020quasibinary,Liu_2024,jaidka2026structurepropertyapplication}

To accelerate the discovery of resilient metastable materials, we introduce a machine learning framework designed to efficiently navigate the TMD chemical space and identify promising candidates for synthesis \citep{dejong2018statistical,Yuan_2025,Griesemer_2023,chen2021leveraginglargescalecomputationaldatabase,Tawfik_2022,Deng_2023,xin2025bridgingtheoryexperimentmaterials}. Our approach bypasses the direct and computationally demanding calculation of elastic properties for every hypothetical structure \citep{Yuan_2025,Jin_2023,Griesemer_2023}. Instead, we train a model to predict a key descriptor of mechanical behavior—Pugh's ratio, $G/K$, the ratio of the shear modulus $G$ to the bulk modulus $K$—from a small set of fundamental electronic and structural features \citep{dejong2018statistical,Jin_2023,stracke2025evaluatingmechanicalpropertyprediction,yue2023highthroughput}. By learning the complex, non-linear relationships between these accessible descriptors and mechanical robustness, our model can rapidly screen large databases of materials to pinpoint those with a high likelihood of being both functional and durable \citep{Deng_2023,Jin_2023,yue2023highthroughput,Griesemer_2023,chen2021leveraginglargescalecomputationaldatabase,dejong2018statistical}.

In this work, we develop a predictive model founded on physically interpretable descriptors, such as the density of states at the Fermi level, which provides insight into the electronic origins of mechanical softening.\citep{Duan_2025,chen2025universalmaterialpropertyprediction,Zhang_2023} We rigorously evaluate our model's ability to generalize to new chemical families using a stringent leave-one-metal-group-out cross-validation scheme, a critical test for any data-driven materials discovery effort aiming to extrapolate beyond its training data.\citep{veillon2025chemicallygroundedevaluationframework} By coupling our model's predictions with robust uncertainty quantification\citep{Guan_2019}, we screen a library of 112 theoretical metastable TMDs.\citep{park2024exploring,Gou_2024} The result is a high-confidence viability map that balances predicted mechanical robustness against thermodynamic accessibility, providing a targeted and prioritized roadmap for the experimental synthesis of novel and resilient functional materials.\citep{park2024exploring,Duan_2025,Zhang_2023,Gou_2024,Gao_2025}
